Agra

Agra is a beautiful city in Uttar Pradesh. It is famous for the Taj Mahal.

Famous Places

The Taj Mahal, Agra Fort and Mehtab Bagh are some famous places in Agra.

Famous Food

Agra is famous for its delicious Petha and Mughlai food.
