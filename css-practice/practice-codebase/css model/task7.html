Typography and Text Styling

The art of making text beautiful and readable

The Importance of Good Typography

Typography is an important part of web design. It helps present information in a clear and attractive way.

Choosing the Right Font

Serif fonts have small decorative strokes at the ends of letters. They are often used in books and articles.

Sans-serif fonts have a clean and modern appearance. They are commonly used on websites and digital screens.

Different font styles create different visual experiences.

Emphasizing Important Text

Good typography improves readability and helps readers understand important information. Always choose styles carefully to keep the page balanced.

Remember that clear and readable text creates a better experience for every reader.
